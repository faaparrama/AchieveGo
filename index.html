WHAT WE HAVE

A descriptive foundation

The existing LPA describes 48,789 students across 108 Dutch schools using three ability and three psychosocial indicators. The main profile differences concern motivation and well-being.

These are group means in standardized units, not diagnostic cutoffs. The demo selects profiles; it does not classify students or establish transfer to US learners.

WHAT STILL NEEDS VALIDATION

The matching layer

No study in this starter set establishes that a particular AchieveGo profile combined with a diagnostic label responds best to a particular intervention. School and specialist teams need to review evidence applicability, delivery, and learner goals.

This prototype uses transparent rules. It does not use an LLM, administer a diagnosis, or learn treatment effects from the LPA.

A TEAM BUILDS THE NEXT VERSION

Research into practice

Researchers appraise evidence and test matching. Educators and specialists specify implementation. Learners and families define priorities and access needs. Engineers maintain provenance, interfaces, and reliable retrieval.

Next: independent evidence review, usability sessions, then a prospective study separating the value of access to evidence from the added value of profile-informed matching.
